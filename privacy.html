Circulate: Reflex Arcade

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be processed when you use Circulate. Last updated: August 28, 2026.

Overview

Circulate is published by Dibu Studios. You do not need to create a traditional account with an email address or password to play. The app may create an anonymous Firebase Authentication identifier so game services can recognize an installation or player record.

Information and services

Depending on the platform, app version, enabled features, and your consent choices, Circulate may use the following services:

  • Firebase Authentication for anonymous authentication.
  • Firebase Firestore for leaderboard and player-related game data.
  • Firebase Analytics and diagnostics, if enabled in the project, to understand app usage, performance, and technical issues.
  • Google AdMob to provide advertising.

These third-party SDKs may process device identifiers, advertising data, coarse location, usage information, and diagnostic information where permitted by the platform and applicable consent settings. The exact information processed can vary by device, region, permissions, and service configuration.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate game features, maintain leaderboards and player progress, understand app performance, diagnose crashes or errors, prevent abuse, and provide or measure advertising. Dibu Studios does not ask players to create a traditional personal account for Circulate.

Third-party privacy policies

Third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Their practices, retention periods, and available privacy controls may differ from ours. You should review the policies provided by Google and the relevant mobile platform for more information about how those services process data.

Your choices

Privacy and advertising choices may be available through the app, your device settings, platform account settings, or consent forms shown in supported regions. Disabling certain permissions or services may affect related features.

Children

Circulate is not designed to request a child's name, email address, or other traditional account details. Platform requirements and third-party service settings may affect how data is handled for younger users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact Dibu Studios at dibustudiosofficial@gmail.com.
